Privacy policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

SH Bridge is a private Android app used by its developer for the developer's own home. It is not published on Google Play and is not offered to other people. This policy describes exactly what the app does with data.

1. Data the app accesses

Only after you sign in with your Google Account and grant access in Google's own consent screen, SH Bridge uses the official Google Home APIs to read information about the Google Home devices you selected:

  • the home (structure) and room names, and the names, types and room of the selected devices;
  • device capabilities and current state (for example on/off, brightness, colour, temperature settings, fan and window-covering position) and changes to that state;
  • connectivity (online/offline) and basic device information that Google provides (manufacturer, product, serial number).

To keep you signed in, the app stores the name of the Google Account you signed in with on the device. It does not read your contacts, location, microphone, camera, messages or other apps.

2. How the data is used

  • To show your devices and their state on your own smart-home dashboard (Home Assistant) running on your own server in your local network.
  • Only when the owner explicitly enables device control (off by default): to carry out commands that the owner gives on that dashboard (lights, plugs, climate units and window coverings) through the official Google Home APIs.

The data is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose.

3. Where the data goes

  • Your own server only. SH Bridge sends the device information to the address of your own server in your local network that you configured. Every message is signed (HMAC-SHA256 with a shared secret) so the server can check that it came from your app and was not changed. Inside the local network this connection uses HTTP with message signing, not TLS encryption; the app allows unencrypted connections only to your own server in the local network.
  • Google. Signing in and reading or controlling devices happens through Google's services. Google processes that data under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • No one else. SH Bridge contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking components and does not send data to any other party.
  • Manual export. The app has an "export inventory" button that opens Android's share menu. Data leaves the device this way only if you tap it and choose a destination yourself.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

SH Bridge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: the data is used only to provide the features described above to the owner, it is not transferred to others, and it is not used for advertising.

6. Keeping, removing and revoking

  • Revoke SH Bridge's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app then can no longer read or control devices.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all data the app stored on the phone.
  • Device information on the owner's server stays there under the owner's control and can be deleted by the owner at any time.

7. Children

SH Bridge is not intended for children and does not knowingly process children's data.

8. Changes and contact
