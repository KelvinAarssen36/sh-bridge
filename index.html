Connect your own Google Home devices to your own private dashboard

SH Bridge is a small, private Android app built for one household: the developer's own home.

What SH Bridge does

  • After you sign in with Google and grant access, it reads the devices you selected in Google Home through the official Google Home APIs: their names, types, rooms, capabilities and current state.
  • It sends that information to your own smart-home server in your own local network (a private Home Assistant dashboard), so the dashboard can show the devices and their state.
  • When the owner explicitly enables it, the dashboard can ask SH Bridge to switch supported devices (lights, plugs, climate units and window coverings) through the same official APIs.

What SH Bridge does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no sale or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No changes to your Google Home setup: it never renames, moves, adds or removes devices or rooms.
  • No unofficial or private Google endpoints, no scraping.
Private app. SH Bridge is not published on Google Play and is not offered as a service to other people. It is used only by its owner for the owner's own home.

Samenvatting (Nederlands)

SH Bridge is een privé-Android-app voor één huishouden. De app leest via de officiële Google Home APIs de apparaten die je in Google Home hebt geselecteerd en stuurt die naar je eigen smart-home-dashboard in je eigen thuisnetwerk. Als de eigenaar dat uitdrukkelijk aanzet, kan het dashboard ook lampen, stekkers, airco's en gordijnen bedienen. Geen reclame, geen analytics, geen tracking en geen delen met derden.

Read the privacy policy for exactly which data is processed, where it is stored and how to remove it.
